About CKD MetaPredict

Our Mission

CKD MetaPredict was created to revolutionize early detection of chronic kidney disease (CKD). Our mission is to empower clinicians and researchers with predictive tools that improve patient outcomes through timely intervention, prevention, and protection.

Research & Technology

At the heart of CKD MetaPredict lies a multilayer stacked machine learning model. This approach combines diverse algorithms to achieve robust consensus, reducing bias and improving accuracy. Our predictor integrates patient demographics and clinical features, producing interpretable predictions with confidence scores that clinicians can trust.

Meet the Team

CKD MetaPredict is led by Pranta Kumar Das, in partnership with the Center for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (CAIR). Our team brings together specialists in data science, artificial intelligence, and software engineering. Each member is driven by a shared vision: applying advanced machine learning to create reliable, interpretable tools that support early detection and better decision-making in healthcare. By combining technical innovation with a focus on usability, the team ensures CKD MetaPredict remains both scientifically rigorous and practically impactful.

Impact & Achievements

CKD MetaPredict has demonstrated significant improvements in prediction accuracy compared to traditional models. By offering interpretable outputs, it bridges the gap between AI research and clinical practice. Our tool has been tested on diverse datasets, achieving high sensitivity and specificity in CKD risk prediction.

Future Roadmap

Looking ahead, we plan to expand CKD MetaPredict to include integration with electronic health records, real-time monitoring dashboards, and mobile-friendly interfaces. We are also exploring partnerships with hospitals and research institutions to validate the model in real-world settings.

Acknowledgments

We gratefully acknowledge the support of CAIR, our research collaborators, and the patients whose anonymized data made this work possible. Their contributions drive our commitment to innovation and patient-centered AI solutions.
